The Asha Way

One Token,
Zero Wait.

We were among the first in AECS Layout to introduce a token-based ordering system. Print your token, pick your counter, and your food is ready before you are.

01

Walk In & Place Your Order

Tell us what you want at the token counter — mix and match from any section.

02

Your Token Gets Printed

Receive a printed token with your number and the counters your food will be at.

03

Go Straight to Your Counter

Head directly to South Indian, Chinese, North Indian, Desserts or Chai — it's already being made.

04

Eat & Enjoy

Pick up your tray, find a seat, and dig in. No queues, no confusion.

Our Story

Built for the
Busy Bengalurean.

Asha Tiffins started with one simple idea — great food shouldn't make you wait. Nestled in the heart of AECS Layout, right beside CMRIT and surrounded by tech parks, we built a tiffin centre that moves as fast as Bengaluru's mornings. Our token system was one of the first of its kind in this neighbourhood, and today it's the reason hundreds of students, engineers, and families trust us every single day.
